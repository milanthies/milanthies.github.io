I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Social Policy Unit at [IDHEAP](https://www.unil.ch/idheap/), the Swiss Graduate School of Public Administration at the University of Lausanne. I work in the [GOVPET Leading House](https://ibb.unisg.ch/en/research-labs/govpet-leading-house/research-program/) on governance in vocational and professional education and training, and at the [Swiss Centre of Expertise in Life Course Research (LIVES)](https://lives.unil.ch/en/page-de-base/about-lives-centre).

My research is in comparative political economy and social policy. It deals with education, skills and labour market policy and, more broadly, with the transformation of states in the context of structural change. I combine quantitative and qualitative methods.

I hold a PhD in Political and Social Sciences from the European University Institute, where I [defended my thesis](https://www.eui.eu/events?id=584867) in March 2026. Before my doctorate I completed a Master of Public Policy at the Hertie School and worked in policy and politics. I have been a visiting researcher at Humboldt University of Berlin and Sciences Po Paris.
