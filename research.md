How do welfare states adapt education and training to structural change, and who gains access to skills as a result? Skills policy has to serve two aims at once: economic upgrading and social inclusion. I study how governments, employers, unions and the EU pursue these aims, and what their choices mean for inequality.

I work with mixed methods. Computational text analysis (topic models, dictionaries and natural language inference) lets me trace policy change across many countries and decades; elite interviews and process tracing explain it in individual cases.
