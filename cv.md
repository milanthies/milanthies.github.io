Milan Thies is a postdoctoral researcher at IDHEAP, University of Lausanne, where he works in the GOVPET Leading House on governance in vocational and professional education and training. He studies the political economy of education, training and the welfare state, with a focus on vocational training, adult education and EU skills policy. His work has been published in *Regulation & Governance* and *Government and Opposition*. He holds a PhD from the European University Institute and previously worked at the German Federal Ministry of Labour and Social Affairs.
